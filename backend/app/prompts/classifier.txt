Some of these are easily confused. Where a pair below applies, decide by WHO
ISSUED the document and WHAT IT DOES, not by the subject it mentions:

LOAN_APPLICATION - a form completed and submitted BY the applicant, asking for
a facility. It carries their declarations and their signature, and it states
what they are requesting.

AGREEMENT - a document issued BY the lender, or executed between the parties,
recording terms that have already been decided: a sanction letter, an offer
letter, a loan agreement. It states what the bank has granted, not what the
applicant has asked for. A letter that refers to an application is not itself
an application.

LEGAL_DOCUMENT - a legal instrument that is not a lending agreement: an
affidavit, a power of attorney, a court order, a notice.

EMPLOYMENT_PROOF - confirms that someone is employed. A document that states a
month's pay in detail is a SALARY_SLIP even when it also proves employment.

The land documents all quote the same property and often the same owner. Tell
them apart by what the document is a record OF:

SALE_DEED - records one transfer of the property from a named seller to a named
buyer, for a stated consideration, registered with the sub-registrar. If the
document conveys the property from one party to another, it is a SALE_DEED.

ENCUMBRANCE_CERTIFICATE - a certificate issued by the registrar listing the
registered transactions affecting the property over a stated period, or
certifying that there were none. It records no transfer of its own; it reports
on other documents, and it always names a period covered.

KHATA_CERTIFICATE - a municipal record certifying whose name the property
stands in for assessment, with a khata or property account number. It certifies
ownership on the municipal register; it does not transfer anything and no money
changes hands.

PROPERTY_TAX_RECEIPT - an acknowledgement that property tax was paid for an
assessment year. It shows an amount paid and a receipt number.

PROPERTY_DOCUMENT - any other land or property paper that is none of the four
above.
